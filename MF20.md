# MF-20

**MF-20** emulates the filter section from the Korg MS-20.

<img src="screenshots/MF-20.png" alt="MF-20 module" height="500">

---

An attempt to bring the distinctive sound of the MS-20 filter to VCV Rack and MetaModule. It emulates both versions of the original:

- **OTA** — the revised MS-20 (late production), based on the LM13600 op-transconductance amplifier. Smooth and open-sounding.
- **Korg35** — the original MS-20, built around Korg's proprietary Korg35 chip. Slightly edgier, with a different distortion character.

Switch between them in the right-click menu. Both self-oscillate cleanly at maximum resonance.

As on the MS-20, the signal runs through the **HP filter first, then the LP filter.** Patch the **Total** cutoff input to sweep both filters together as a band or notch, exactly the way the MS-20's shared cutoff modulation works. Turn up **Drive** to add saturation and harmonic color. 

MF-20 supports polyphony on both VCV Rack and MetaModule.

---

## Controls

- The cutoff knobs are log-scaled 20 Hz – 20 kHz.
- The resonance, or "Peak" knobs self-oscillate at 100%.
- At minimum Drive the filter is clean; turning it up drives the input into soft clipping.
- The attenuverters scale the CV inputs ±1×.

The two filters are always independent at the knob level. To sweep them together, modulate the **Total** cutoff input (see below).

## Technical notes

Both modes use a TPT (Topology-Preserving Transform / zero-delay feedback) implementation, discretizing the full second-order system in one step.

**OTA mode** — models the LM13600-based topology analyzed by Stinchcombe (2006). Diode saturation is in the resonance feedback path; piecewise-linear approximation gives a closed-form solution per sample with no Newton iteration. Pre-gain applies √drive amplification with a ±5 V soft-clip threshold before the filter.

**Korg35 mode** — models the original Korg35 chip (C1 = 3.3 nF, C2 = 1 nF, 3:1 ratio → damping constant 8/3). Nonlinearity is in the forward path (input), not the feedback. Asymmetric clipping thresholds (T_pos = 1.0, T_neg = 0.85 normalized) create even-order harmonics matching the hardware's asymmetric resonance behavior described in Stinchcombe §4; each clip corner is rounded with a small quadratic knee so the curve has a continuous slope, which cuts the aliasing a hard corner would fold back at high drive and cutoff. The Drive control clips the forward path rather than the resonance feedback.
